For a first trip to Greece, spend two nights in Athens, take a ferry to Mykonos and another to Santorini, then fly back to Athens for your flight home. Budget roughly ₹1.4 to ₹2.1 lakh per person for eight days including flights, and start the Schengen visa at least six weeks before you fly.

## Visa and entry rules for Indians

**Indian passport holders need a Schengen short-stay visa (type C) for tourism in Greece, applied for in advance.** The Greek Embassy in New Delhi's [visa pages](https://www.mfa.gr/missionsabroad/en/india-en/visas/processing-time-of-applications-for-schengen-visa.html) say, in the text we could read on 10 October 2026:

1. **Where to apply:** at a VFS Global visa application centre in India; the embassy in New Delhi assesses every application.
2. **How early:** no more than six months before the planned trip.
3. **Processing:** up to 15 working days from when the embassy receives an admissible application, extended to a maximum of 45 days in individual cases such as extra documents or an interview.
4. **Delivery:** it takes some days for your file to reach the embassy from the centre: about 5 days for New Delhi and 6 to 8 days for the rest of India. The embassy says it is not liable if you apply less than 15 days (plus delivery days) before travel, and that applications peak from April to July.
5. **Fee:** the standard Schengen fee is €90 for adults, €45 for ages 6 to under 12, and free under 6, in force since June 2024. VFS charges a service fee on top, which we could not confirm from an official page; read it off the VFS Greece India page before booking.

The Greek foreign ministry site refused our automated fetch, so we relied on the embassy text as returned by search and on the Swiss federal fee table, which restates the Schengen amounts. We also could not load the embassy's document checklist, so follow the one on the embassy and VFS pages exactly. In practice it covers flight and hotel reservations, travel insurance with the minimum Schengen cover, bank statements and tax returns.

> Apply only through VFS Global or the embassy. Look-alike "Greece visa" sites charge large fees for the same appointment. Keep flights on a refundable fare until the visa is in hand.

## Day-by-day 8-day Greece itinerary

### Day 1: arrive in Athens

Stay near **Plaka** or **Monastiraki** so the Acropolis, the metro and the airport train are close. Walk up **Filopappou Hill** for a first look at the Acropolis at sunset.

### Day 2: the Acropolis and old Athens

Go to the **Acropolis** at opening time to beat the heat and the tour buses, and book the ticket online. Spend the afternoon at the **Acropolis Museum**, then eat in **Psyrri** or **Koukaki**. Wear shoes with grip: the marble is slippery.

### Day 3: ferry to Mykonos

Take a morning ferry from **Piraeus** or **Rafina** to Mykonos; the trip takes from about two and a half to five hours depending on the boat. Book a seat in advance in season. Spend the evening in **Mykonos Town** (Chora) and watch the sun set from **Little Venice**.

### Day 4: Delos and beaches

Take the short boat to **Delos**, the ancient island sanctuary, in the morning, then spend the afternoon at a quieter beach such as **Agios Sostis** or **Ornos**. The famous party beaches are loud and expensive; you can skip them.

### Day 5: ferry to Santorini

Ferries between the two islands take about two hours. Arrive in the afternoon, check in, and head to **Oia** for sunset. Be there an hour early in season, because the best spots fill up.

### Day 6: Fira and the caldera

Walk the caldera rim from **Fira** to **Imerovigli**, a few hours with views the whole way. In the afternoon take a **caldera cruise** past the volcano and hot springs, the most common boat trip.

### Day 7: Akrotiri and the south

Visit **Akrotiri**, the Bronze Age town buried by the volcano, then the **Red Beach** area and a winery on the way back. Hire a car or ATV only if you are confident on narrow roads; buses and taxis are simpler.

### Day 8: fly back to Athens

Take an early flight from Santorini to Athens, about 45 minutes, and connect to your flight home. Book the international flight for the evening or later, never the same morning, because ferries and flights both get cancelled in strong winds.

## Island-hopping tips

- **Meltemi winds:** the strong north winds of July and August can cancel ferries. Keep a buffer day at the end and favour a plane on the last leg.
- **Ferries:** compare operators on a ferry search site and book a few weeks ahead in season. Fast boats cost more but cut the journey time.
- **Where to stay:** on Santorini, Fira and Imerovigli cost less than Oia and sit on the same caldera path. On Mykonos, Chora is walkable; the beach areas need a bus or taxi.

## Best time to go

May, June, September and early October are the sweet spots: warm sea, daily ferries and lower prices than July and August. Many island hotels and restaurants close between November and March, and ferries thin out, so winter trips work better in Athens only.

## Tips for Indian travellers

- **Vegetarian and Jain food:** Greek salad, spanakopita, gemista, fava, grilled cheese and tzatziki are easy. Ask about meat stock in beans and stuffed vegetables. Onion and garlic are common.
- **Money:** carry a forex card or multi-currency card and use contactless payment. Don't rely on UPI here; keep some euro cash for small tavernas and taxis.
- **SIM and plugs:** an eSIM bought before you fly avoids roaming bills. Greece uses Type C and Type F plugs, so a Type C adapter works.
- **Heat:** summer afternoons are hot and the sun is strong; carry water and a hat, and plan the Acropolis early.
