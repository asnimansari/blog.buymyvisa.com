Iceland looks like the backdrop to a hundred Bollywood songs because it is: waterfalls you can walk behind, black-sand beaches, glaciers calving into a lagoon, and in winter the northern lights. It's also the most expensive and most paperwork-heavy trip on this site. Here's how to do a week well, starting with the visa.

## Visa and entry rules for Indians

**You need a Schengen short-stay (type C) visa.** Iceland is in the Schengen area, and in India its applications go through [VFS Global](https://visa.vfsglobal.com/ind/en/isl). If Iceland is your only (or main) Schengen country, apply for Iceland's visa, not another country's.

1. **Book a VFS appointment** online for biometrics, at least **15 days** and up to **six months** before you travel. Summer slots go fast.
2. **Fees:** the €90 visa fee, paid in rupees at the centre (**₹9,810** at our last check), plus the VFS service charge (**₹3,161**, paid online when you book).
3. **Documents:**
   - your passport, valid three months beyond your return and issued in the last ten years;
   - the application form and photos;
   - flight and hotel bookings;
   - **travel insurance** covering at least €30,000;
   - your last few months' bank statements and income tax returns;
   - a leave letter or company letter (or proof of business or study).
4. **Processing:** usually about **15 calendar days** after your appointment, and longer in peak season.

<p class="note">
  VFS can refuse to book an appointment if your passport expires soon. Renew it first if it has less than
  about a year left.
</p>

> Fees and VFS charges change. Check the [VFS Iceland page](https://visa.vfsglobal.com/ind/en/isl) before you book, and use only that site, never an agent's look-alike.

Carry an **International Driving Permit** with your Indian licence: most rental companies ask for one. Book automatic cars early; they're the first to go.

## Day-by-day itinerary

### Day 1: arrive in Reykjavík

Pick up your car at Keflavík airport and drive 45 minutes to **Reykjavík**. Walk up to **Hallgrímskirkja**, see the **Harpa** concert hall, and eat at the harbour. If you land early, the **Blue Lagoon** is close to the airport (book ahead), or save it for the last day.

### Day 2: the Golden Circle

A 230 km loop:

- **Þingvellir National Park**, where you can walk between the North American and Eurasian plates;
- the **Strokkur geyser**, which erupts every few minutes;
- the two-tier **Gullfoss** waterfall.

End the day at a geothermal pool (the **Secret Lagoon** at Flúðir), then sleep near **Hella** or **Hvolsvöllur**.

### Day 3: south coast waterfalls

Walk behind **Seljalandsfoss** (bring a rain jacket), find the hidden **Gljúfrabúi** next door, then continue to **Skógafoss**. On sunny days its spray throws a rainbow, and 500-odd steps lead to the top.

### Day 4: Vík and the black-sand beaches

Visit **Dyrhólaey**'s arch and puffins (May to August), then **Reynisfjara** beach with its basalt columns and the **Reynisdrangar** sea stacks. **Stay well back from the water**: Reynisfjara's "sneaker waves" have killed visitors. Sleep in **Vík**.

### Day 5: the glacier lagoon

Drive east across the lava fields to **Jökulsárlón**, where icebergs from the Breiðamerkurjökull glacier float out to sea, and cross the road to **Diamond Beach**, where they wash up on black sand. Book a guided **glacier walk** at Skaftafell or, from November to March, an **ice-cave** tour.

### Days 6–7: back west

It's a long drive back (about five hours to Reykjavík without stops), so break it at the waterfalls you skipped, or at **Reykjadalur**, a hike to a hot river you can bathe in. Spend your last morning at the **Blue Lagoon** or **Sky Lagoon** before the flight home.

## Best time to visit

**June to August** has long days (the midnight sun in June), open roads and puffins, but the highest prices. **September** is the sweet spot: lower prices, autumn colours and the first northern lights. **October to March** is aurora season, with short days and icy roads. Drive only if you're confident on ice, or take tours.

## Getting there from India

There are no direct flights. The easiest routes connect through **Amsterdam**, **Copenhagen**, **Frankfurt** or **Helsinki** onto Icelandair or another European airline, or through a Gulf hub to Europe and on. Allow 14 to 18 hours each way. Make sure your Schengen visa covers the date you first enter the Schengen area, which may be your transit airport, not Iceland. A non-Schengen hub such as London can need a separate transit visa, so check before you book.
