Vietnam has become the trip Indians recommend to each other: direct four-hour flights, an online visa, and a country that packs a limestone bay, a lantern-lit old town and two very different big cities into one week. This route flies north to south, so you never backtrack.

## Visa and entry rules for Indians

**You need an e-visa, applied for online before you fly.** Apply only at the official portal, [evisa.gov.vn](https://evisa.gov.vn/).

1. **Fee:** US$25 for a single entry or US$50 for multiple entries, paid by card online. It is not refunded if the visa is refused.
2. **Stay:** up to **90 days**. The visa's dates are fixed, so enter your real arrival date and port.
3. **Processing:** usually three to five working days. Apply two to three weeks ahead to be safe.
4. **Documents:** a passport valid for at least **six months** from arrival, a passport-style photo and a scan of your passport's photo page.

Print the approval PDF and carry it with your passport; there's no stamp or sticker in advance.

> Dozens of agency sites look official and charge two or three times the fee. If the address isn't **evisa.gov.vn**, it isn't the government.

## Day-by-day itinerary

### Days 1–2: Hanoi

Stay in the **Old Quarter**. Walk around **Hoan Kiem Lake**, see the **Temple of Literature**, and have egg coffee at a café up a narrow staircase. On day 2, watch the trains squeeze through the houses on **Train Street** (check whether it's open to visitors), then take a cooking class or a food tour in the evening.

### Day 3: Ha Long Bay (or Lan Ha Bay)

Take a transfer to the bay (about 2 h 30 m on the expressway) for an **overnight cruise** among nearly 2,000 limestone islands: kayak through caves, swim off the boat, and wake up to the karsts in the mist. **Lan Ha Bay**, next door, has fewer boats and is worth asking your operator about.

### Days 4–6: Hoi An

Fly Hanoi to **Da Nang** and drive 45 minutes to **Hoi An**. The old town is a UNESCO World Heritage site of yellow merchant houses, and at night the river and streets glow with silk lanterns. Get clothes tailored in 24 hours, cycle out to **An Bang beach**, and take a day trip to the **My Son** temple ruins or the **Marble Mountains**.

### Days 7–8: Ho Chi Minh City (Saigon)

Fly Da Nang to **Ho Chi Minh City**. See the **War Remnants Museum**, the post office and Notre-Dame cathedral, then eat your way through District 1 and District 4 by motorbike on a guided street-food tour. On the last morning, take a half-day trip to the **Cu Chi tunnels** or the **Mekong Delta** before your flight home.

## Best time to visit

**February to April** is the best compromise for a north-to-south trip: mild in Hanoi, dry in Hoi An, hot but bearable in Saigon. **Avoid October to December** in central Vietnam (heavy rain and typhoons in Hoi An). **December to February** is cold and grey in the north. Bring a jacket for a Ha Long cruise.

## Getting there from India

IndiGo, VietJet and Vietnam Airlines fly direct from Delhi and Mumbai (and some other metros) to Hanoi and Ho Chi Minh City in about four to five hours. Book an **open-jaw** ticket, into Hanoi and out of Saigon, so the itinerary never doubles back.
